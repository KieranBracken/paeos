## 3. Key Unresolved Boundary & Identity Questions

### Question 1: SAKG vs. Concept OS Knowledge Boundaries
- **Issue**: SAKG represents system architecture, mechanisms, and causal rules. Concept OS represents concept graphs, semantic proximity, and reasoning.
- **Investigation Needed**: Where does architectural knowledge end and conceptual reasoning begin? Should SAKG be a specialized subgraph inside Concept OS, or a distinct lower-level substrate?

### Question 2: Orca-strator vs. `cli_orchestrator` vs. PAEOS Governance
- **Issue**: Three separate orchestration mechanisms exist: `orca-strator`, `cli_orchestrator`, and PAEOS `SoftLoop`.
- **Investigation Needed**: Does PAEOS own constitutional execution governance while Orca-strator owns user-land agent collaboration, and `cli_orchestrator` serves merely as the CLI harness?

### Question 3: The Fate of Library of Fire
- **Issue**: `Library Of Fire` exists as an empty placeholder repo, while its original inspiration engine concepts were built into `Concept Graph `.
- **Investigation Needed**: Is Library of Fire required as an immutable corpus/document storage layer, or should it be formally retired/merged into SAKG/KAE?

### Question 4: Sentium Core vs. Subsystems
- **Issue**: `sentium_paper_trading` and `cli_orchestrator` are separate repos.
- **Investigation Needed**: What is the irreducible core of Sentium? Is paper-trading simply an execution adapter under the broader Sentium Autonomous Trading Platform?
