## 1. Contradiction 1: Knowledge Representation (SAKG vs Concept OS vs Library of Fire)
- **Contradiction**: SAKG, Concept OS, and Library of Fire all claim knowledge representation responsibilities.
- **Resolution Matrix**:
  - `SAKG`: Owns concrete **causal engineering principles, structural constraints, and architectural laws**.
  - `Concept OS`: Owns abstract **semantic proximity, conceptual manifold coordinates, analogies, and concept blending**.
  - `Library of Fire`: Retired as an independent system; document ingestion unified into Knowledge Acquisition Engine (KAE), storing raw text in CAS.

## 2. Contradiction 2: Multi-Agent Orchestration (PAEOS vs Orca-strator vs `cli_orchestrator`)
- **Contradiction**: Three separate systems implement agent orchestration.
- **Resolution Matrix**:
  - `PAEOS`: Owns **constitutional execution governance, verification court, CAS state, and self-construction soft-loop**.
  - `Orca-strator`: Owns **interactive user-land agent councils, model provider dashboard, and GUI cockpit**.
  - `cli_orchestrator`: Consolidated into Sentium Core as its CLI operational harness.

## 3. Contradiction 3: Inspiration Engine vs Concept OS
