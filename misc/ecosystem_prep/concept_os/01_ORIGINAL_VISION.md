## 1. Primary Purpose & Definition

Concept OS is a foundational research vision for an **Operating System for Discovery**, investigating whether reasoning, discovery, and knowledge creation can be represented as a universal computational operating system.

Key Premise:
> *"Discovery itself may be computable. Intelligence is a process, not a model. Knowledge is active, capable of evolving, reorganizing, explaining itself, and generating hypotheses."*

Processes coordinated:
- Representation & Abstraction
- Analogy & Concept Blending
- Constrained Search through Concept Space
- Verification & Experimentation
- Self-Improving Discovery Engine
