# DEBT-0117 — Autonomous Gap Adaptation and Delegated Self-Remediation capability not yet physically demonstrated

Status: **OPEN** · Filed: 2026-09-09 · Channel: CER-3  
Priority: **NOT P1-BLOCKING** — see phasing in Section 23 of referenced doctrine  
Scope: `PRE_P2_AUTONOMY_READINESS_GATE` and P2–P7; P1 implementation and qualification are not expanded by this entry.

## Compromise

PAEOS contains Architect, Council, critique, Court, self-hosting, Acceptance Governance, and
L17–L19 learning machinery, but the end-to-end capability to detect a novel engineering gap,
classify it, establish delegated authority, autonomously redesign/remediate itself, independently
verify the remediation, extract the generalized failure class, durably preserve recursive
blocker/remediation lineages via an append-only Mission Dependency Graph (MDG), and resume the
interrupted parent mission has not yet been physically demonstrated.

The system can implement governed missions under Founder direction; it cannot yet autonomously
identify a previously unseen deficiency in itself, classify that deficiency against the delegated
authority ladder, govern a full recursive remediation tree, survive process crashes during deep
execution, and safely unwind back to the root mission — all without routine Founder intervention.

## Reason

The autonomous-adaptation capability is intentionally deferred. P1 concerns are restricted to the
minimum trust prerequisites: deterministic acceptance readiness, trustworthy worker boundaries,
physical evidence chains, candidate identity, independent audit, promotion binding, and
Founder-stop correctness. Implementing end-to-end autonomous adaptation now would expand P1
scope without improving P1 trustworthiness.

## Ideal solution

**Target state:** Novel engineering gaps within existing delegated authority are ordinarily
remediated without Founder intervention. Founder escalation occurs only when the decision
genuinely exceeds delegated authority or enters a Founder-reserved constitutional/sovereign
domain.

The complete capability requires:

1. **Mission Dependency Graph (MDG) Architecture:**
   - Append-only Ledger is the sole authority; derived SQLite/in-memory projections are rebuildable.
   - Preserves `root_mission_id`, `parent_task_id`, `nesting_depth`, `blocking_chain`, and typed
     edges (`BLOCKS`, `SPAWNED_BY`, `REMEDIATES`, `DEPENDS_ON`, `RESUMES`, `SUPERSEDES`).
   - Reconstructs deterministically after PAEOS daemon restart, worker death, or provider failure.
2. **Root-Envelope Authority Model:**
   - $\text{ChildAuthority} \subseteq \text{RootMissionAuthorizedEnvelope} \cap \text{DelegatedAuthorityCeiling}$.
   - Children cannot broaden their own authority, but Authority Resolver may derive child-specific
     scopes differing from immediate parent's task-local scope if contained within root mission envelope.
   - Deep recursion alone is not an escalation trigger; Founder stop is reserved for genuine A4/A5 boundaries.
3. **Ceremony-Depth Selection (L02 Law):**
   - Trace-A remediation auto-discharges applicable canonical obligations with runtime evidence.
   - Trace-B remediation receives full ceremony.
   - Recursive remediation does not bypass L01–L19 and does not defeat Trace-A/Trace-B economy.
4. **Deterministic Cycle Rejection & Recursion Circuit Breaker:**
   - Topological checks reject cyclic dependencies before ledger commit (`CycleDetectedError`).
   - Configurable recursion circuit breaker pauses branch for RCA, replanning, and Council before
     any Founder escalation.
5. **Autonomous Recovery from Child Failure:**
   - Exhausted retry budgets trigger alternative remedies, task recompilation, model rotation, or
     parent-level replanning before Founder intervention.
6. **Exact-Once Resume & Sibling Blocker Aggregation:**
   - Suspended parents resume only after all sibling blockers are extinguished.
   - Monotonic fencing tokens and CAS checkpoint validation guarantee exact-once execution under replay.
7. **Advisory Weakness Finder Probes:**
   - Probes `WF-MDG-01` through `WF-MDG-09` continuously audit graph health without direct mutation authority.
8. **Orca Lineage Projection:**
   - Read-only lineage projection (`GET /api/v1/missions/{id}/lineage`) without lock contention or governance bypass.
9. **Maturity Scenarios (Doctrine Section 18):**
   - Scenario A: Novel bounded gap → fully autonomous end-to-end remediation.
   - Scenario B: Constitutional-boundary gap → correct autonomous STOP and decision packet.
   - Scenario C: Historical scar recurrence → earlier detection than original discovery.
   - Scenario D: Weakness Finder discovery before mission failure → governed remediation without self-authorization.
   - Scenario E: Recursive multi-level blocker remediation and deterministic unwind ($A \to B \to C \to D \to E$).
   - Scenario F: Lawful ceremony-depth selection in nested remediation (Trace-A auto-discharge + Trace-B full ceremony).

## Mandatory pre-P3 end-to-end autonomy integration proof

**Status: RECORDED / NOT EXECUTED.** Founder directive of 2026-09-23 adds a bounded
pre-P3 physical composition gate to this existing debt; it does not create a new
autonomy subsystem, lifecycle layer, or debt item. Sequence:
`DEBT-0258 stable/qualified` → `DEBT-0260 qualified` → remaining pre-P3
hardening, including this proof and DEBT-0249 → substantial P3. The qualified
PRE-P2 substrate scenarios remain valid historical evidence, but do not by
themselves prove this continuous engineering-remediation loop.

**Single continuous governed scenario.** Start a real parent engineering mission
through the normal production entrypoint. During execution, inject or expose an
ordinary novel defect whose solution is not pre-scripted. PAEOS must physically
detect and classify it as a `CURRENT_BLOCKER` (not a nonblocking finding), derive
its A0–A3 authority where lawful, durably checkpoint and suspend the parent,
and commit exact MDG blocker/remediation lineage. A governed child must use
existing L03–L11 Architect/Council/critique reasoning to distinguish local
repair, mechanism redesign, bounded architectural redesign, and primitive-level
correction as applicable; freeze acceptance; implement under L12; and obtain
independent verification/Court/A-08 evidence as required. No actor may expand
its own authority or certify its own work.

Exercise a real recursive branch in that same mission: parent encounters blocker
A → remediation child encounters novel blocker B → governed grandchild qualifies
→ child resumes **exactly once** and qualifies → parent resumes **exactly once**
and continues the original root mission. Preserve root envelope, run/candidate,
checkpoint, fencing, and MDG identities throughout. At a meaningful point in
this branch, kill the controlling PAEOS process; restart through the normal
production entrypoint with no retained process memory. Recovery must reconstruct
the same lineage and continue without Codex-chat prompting, Founder “continue”
input, manual repair, duplicate remediation/resume, stale evidence, or silent
authority expansion. The external chat/session is not the convergence controller.

Independently returned **REMAND** is an autonomous transition, not a terminal
Founder handoff. Physically exercise at least one genuine failed-invariant
remand and show PAEOS ingesting and reproducing it, classifying its root cause,
creating or updating the governed remediation branch, repairing or replanning,
reverifying and resubmitting, then qualifying the child, re-evaluating the
original blocker, and resuming the parent without Founder prompting. Do not
inject a verdict or count a model-authored assertion as independent review.
Ordinary implementation/verifier failures, bounded redesign, recursive
difficulty, and authorized provider substitution must follow bounded recovery
rather than a Founder stop. A separate authentic negative control must show that
a genuine A4/A5 or other Founder-reserved condition **does** stop with the
required decision packet.

**Acceptance evidence:** bind physical production-entrypoint traces, append-only
ledger/MDG and immutable checkpoint hashes, exact authority/capability envelopes,
L03–L12 contract/candidate lineage, independent remand and final verification
records, controller-death/restart observations, and exact-once resume counts to
one root mission. Negative controls must reject duplicate recovery, stale
lineage/evidence, unauthorized scope growth, synthetic review, and bypass of a
Founder stop. If any link requires manual chat coordination or cannot be
reconciled from durable state, this pre-P3 gate remains **UNQUALIFIED**.

This is the minimum pre-P3 composition proof. P7 retains deeper adaptive and
adversarial failure-injection verification; P8 retains the full zero-handholding
Founder journey; P9 retains the independent whole-estate audit and seal. None is
discharged or weakened by this earlier proof. `SENTIUM_FROZEN` remains in force.

## Estimated impact

Until repaid, novel engineering gaps within PAEOS's lawful delegated authority will continue to
require Founder intervention regardless of whether Founder involvement is constitutionally
necessary. Furthermore, nested blocker side-tracks risk losing the original parent mission context
upon restart. This is an autonomy and resiliency defect but does not presently block P1
qualification, which concerns itself with trustworthy governed execution under Founder direction
rather than unsupervised recursive adaptation.
