# DEBT-0062 — Historical Outcome backfill is hand-attributed

Status: **OPEN** · Filed: 2026-09-02 · Channel: CER-3 · Priority: **MEDIUM**
Task: `SAKG-1/I-BACKFILL`
decision_id: sakg://paeos-recovery/Decision/01J00000000000000000000F01

## Compromise

Historical debt and incident records are backfilled as candidate Outcome nodes before the S3
automatic outcome loop exists. The source record is retained by SHA-256 and the Outcome is bound
to the Decision named above, but the historical attribution is **hand-attributed**, not inferred
from automatically observed execution telemetry.

## Reason

SAEOS-1 §8 requires Outcome memory for S2 while the automatic outcome loop is an S3 capability.
Waiting for S3 would leave the graph unable to explain whether sealed Decisions held in practice.

## Ideal solution

The S3 automatic loop should observe post-seal execution, reconcile the observation against the
Decision and its falsifier, and append an evidence-derived Outcome with independent provenance.

## Repayment conditions

The compromise is repaid when the S3 automatic loop emits candidate-bound, immutable observations,
joins them to the same Decision, and an independent audit verifies that observed Outcomes replace
neither the source record nor the hand-attributed historical node. Until then these nodes remain
`status=candidate`, `hand_attributed=true`, and `evidence_derived=false`.

## Risk

Hand attribution can preserve a mistaken historical interpretation or overstate whether a
Decision held. Consumers MUST treat these Outcomes as untrusted candidate memory and MUST NOT
promote them based on frequency, ranking, or the presence of this record alone.

## SAKG-1/I-BACKFILL acceptance

This debt entry is the explicit CER-3 record required by Task 14 and remains open until the S3
automatic loop and its independent evidence are available.
