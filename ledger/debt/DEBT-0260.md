## Compromise

PAEOS can move from a plausible local symptom or mechanism diagnosis into L12 implementation without first establishing whether the underlying architectural primitives can support the claimed guarantee. Successive implementation/remand cycles can therefore discover one missing boundary or primitive at a time. Existing semantic-scope, architecture-conformance, and Court controls do not by themselves prove pre-build *primitive sufficiency* across external side effects, persistence, concurrency, and recovery.

The three diagnostic levels must remain distinct:

1. `LEVEL_1_SYMPTOM`: an observed failure, such as Builder recovery after a crash failing.
2. `LEVEL_2_MECHANISM`: the immediate causal interaction, such as disagreement between SQLite and an external monotonic anchor.
3. `LEVEL_3_PRIMITIVE`: the missing architectural capability, such as no durable idempotent Builder job identity spanning the external execution side effect.

For applicable systemic/high-risk work, a Level 1 or Level 2 explanation alone must not establish L12 readiness. The analysis must establish sufficiency, identify a specific primitive deficit, or explicitly justify why no deeper primitive is needed.

## Reason and motivating scar

DEBT-0258 v1–v3 reviews and the v4 shadow candidate exposed three independently durable boundaries: the PAEOS governance ledger, the external monotonic anchor, and the Builder executor/runtime. A local `BUILDER_STARTED = true` event cannot establish that a durable Builder job was accepted, can be queried after an uncertain acknowledgement, or can resume the *same* logical job after restart. The DEBT-0258 review consequently derived `DURABLE_IDEMPOTENT_BUILDER_JOB`: stable transaction/job identities, idempotent acceptance, independently queryable durable receipt, lineage/fence and anchor bindings, restart-resumable state, and output provenance. DEBT-0258 remains the motivating scar and a required negative regression; its current shadow candidate is **REMAND / NOT READY FOR LIVE TRANSACTION**, not qualification evidence.

The new debt does **not** absorb DEBT-0258 remediation or change its authority boundary. It is PAEOS engineering-process capability, not an Orca feature, separate ecosystem product, or new lifecycle layer.
