# DEBT-0103 — In-Place Mutation of Intake Manifest Scopes Destroys Declared Provenance

Status: **OPEN** · Filed: 2026-08-08 · Channel: CER-3
Level: **runtime-selfhost** (touches `runtime/selfhost.py`).
Source: Checkpoint Audit Loop (Constitutional Drift Audit).

## 1. Observation

In `_parse_intake` (`runtime/selfhost.py`), `intake.plan_write_scopes` and `intake.changed_paths` are overwritten with the output of `infer_write_scopes(...)`.

Overwriting human- or planner-declared intake parameters during parsing destroys provenance. Downstream runtime components cannot distinguish between what the intake author explicitly authorized and what the runtime engine heuristically inferred.

## 2. Root Cause

Destructive reassignment of intake fields during JSON parsing instead of maintaining explicit vs. inferred scope distinction.

## 3. Remediation Strategy

- **Short-term:** Store inferred scopes separately or validate inferred scopes against explicit declarations without mutating `plan_write_scopes` and `changed_paths`.
- **Long-term:** Preserve strict immutability of parsed backlog intake manifests.
